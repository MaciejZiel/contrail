`land.json` and `borders.json` are derived from Natural Earth 1:110m vector data
(`ne_110m_land`, `ne_110m_admin_0_boundary_lines_land`), which is in the public domain
(https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). Coordinates are rounded to two decimals.
They back the offline basemap used by demo mode, CI and screenshots.

`earth-day.jpg` (NASA Blue Marble, shaded relief and bathymetry) and `earth-night.jpg` (NASA Black Marble, VIIRS night lights)
were exported as 4096x2048 equirectangular images from NASA GIBS (https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov/). NASA imagery is not
copyrighted; credit: NASA Earth Observatory / NASA GIBS. They are the surface of the globe at low zoom.
