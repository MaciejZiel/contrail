`land.json` and `borders.json` are derived from Natural Earth 1:110m vector data
(`ne_110m_land`, `ne_110m_admin_0_boundary_lines_land`), which is in the public domain
(https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). Coordinates are rounded to two decimals.
They back the offline basemap used by demo mode, CI and screenshots.
